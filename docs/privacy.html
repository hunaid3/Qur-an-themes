Privacy Policy

Qur'an Themes for Android · Effective 3 November 2026

Qur'an Themes is an offline app for reading the Qur'an by theme. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device. All of its content (Qur'an text, translations and tafsir) is built into the app.

What is stored on your device

To make reading easier, the app saves a few settings on your phone only:

  • your reading settings, such as the translation and Arabic text size you chose;
  • which themes you have opened, and the last theme you read, so the app can show your progress and let you continue.

This information never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include these settings in your device backup, under Google's own privacy terms. The developer has no access to these backups.

Links to other apps

If you tap a link that leads outside the app, it opens in another app, such as your browser. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
